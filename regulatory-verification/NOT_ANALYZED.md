# Evidence items M5 does not examine

Milestone 5 slice 1 ships verification profiles for exactly two evidence items:

- `S02-FORM-1` — Factory Form No. 1 (S-02)
- `F02-FORM-B` — FSSAI Form B (F-02)

Every other document-upload requirement in the M4 registry is reported as
`NOT_ANALYZED`. That is a deliberate statement, not a gap left unmarked: the
system does not examine these files and does not imply anything about them.

Writing a profile requires anchor text and field targets that can be traced to
a source in this repository or to a real specimen of the document. Where
neither exists, no profile is written. Inventing anchors would mean inventing
regulatory facts.

Non-upload items (`FORM_INPUT`, `DECLARATION`, `FEE`, `INSPECTION_EVENT`) are
outside M5's scope entirely and are not listed here.
